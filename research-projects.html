PUBBS Transit v1.0

Service Level Optimization Between Public Bus and Paratransit Services Along a Transport Corridor

Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs 2015 – 2019

Project Objectives

  1. Determine the passenger travel demand including a modal split model between public transit and para-transit services.
  2. Determine feasible range of service delivery levels for transit services. This essentially has three components:
    1. To determine the Level of Service ranges for bus transit and para-transit services based on users' perception in the Indian context
    2. To determine the expected service levels of users across various user groups
    3. To determine service levels that provides maximum marginal utility to the service providers
  3. Determine the optimal level of service for public bus transport and para-transit (in terms of fleet size, vehicle capacity, headway, etc.) during peak and off-peak hours.
  4. Development of a framework for regulation and implementation.

PUBBS Transit v2.0

An Intelligent Urban Bus Transit Dispatch, Control and Surveillance System using Artificial Intelligence and User Perception

Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs 2019 – 2023

Project Description

This project aims to develop a fully automated bus dispatch, control and surveillance system using artificial intelligence by integrating predicted passenger demand, passenger expectations, operator's resources and historical data collected using ETM and AVL systems and through primary surveys.

  1. Collection and storage of GPS location data from bus tracking systems, electronic ticketing data of different routes and other relevant data.
  2. Development of algorithms and software for predicting:
    1. Future transit passenger travel demand for bus routes (Passenger arrival rate at bus stops, Passenger O-D at each bus stop)
      • Long term transit passenger travel demand
      • Short term transit passenger travel demand variation
    2. Expected bus arrival times at bus stops at different times of the day (using historical data, real time data on bus location, expected passenger boarding alighting count at bus stops and other information).
  3. Development of models for determining route wise service schedule (frequency and stop wise timetable), bus schedules and crew requirement during service hours for bus operations.
  4. Development of algorithms and service strategies for automated bus dispatch, control and surveillance system considering disruptions in normal service and other exogenous factors.
