Pubbs Campus System

An end-to-end campus mobility ecosystem designed to simplify operations, optimize fleet performance, and deliver reliable transportation experiences across your institution.

Pubbs Campus is an integrated campus transportation management platform that connects students, conductors, buses, and administrators through a unified digital ecosystem.

The platform aims to replace manual campus bus operations with a smarter, data-driven transport system that improves accessibility, efficiency, and commuter experience.
