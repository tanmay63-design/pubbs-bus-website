Campus Bus Service

Campus Bus Service Information Guide

The Campus Bus Service has been introduced to provide convenient and accessible transportation across the IIT Kharagpur campus, connecting major academic, residential, administrative and other important campus facilities. The service is available to students, faculty, staff, residents and other members of the campus community. Buses operate on three designated loop routes, covering key locations and designated bus stops across the campus. The sections below provide detailed route alignments, designated stops and scheduled service timings, along with guidance on accessing and using the service.

Monday – Friday 7:15 AM – 7:15 PM
Saturday, Sunday & Holidays 4:00 PM – 9:00 PM

Each route covers the specified stops as per the approved route plan. Buses run on designated loop routes, covering all scheduled stops.

Bus Pass Options

To avail the Campus Bus Service, users are required to purchase a valid bus pass. The following pass options are available.

Daily Pass Rs.15 10 trips per day
Weekly Pass Rs.80 Valid for 7 days
Monthly Pass Rs.300 Valid for 30 days
Semester Pass Rs.1200 Full semester access

Guide to Using the PUBBS Transit Campus App

1
Download & Sign Up / Log In

Download and install the PUBBS Transit Campus App and complete the registration or login process.

2
View Routes & Bus Location

Open the route section to view available routes and designated stops. The app displays the live location of buses on the selected routes, so you can track the approaching bus.

3
Select a Pass

Go to the “My Tickets” section and select the required pass. The applicable pass options and fares are displayed in the application.

4
Purchase the Pass

For paid passes, complete the payment directly through the app. Once payment is successful, the pass becomes available in the app.

5
Show the QR Code

The active pass contains a QR code, which must be presented for validation while boarding the bus.

6
Validate the Pass

Validate the QR code by scanning it through the QR scanner installed in the bus, or by getting it scanned by the conductor through the conductor application. Ensure the QR code is successfully validated before travelling.

If a pass cannot be purchased through the app, the conductor / helper onboard the bus can issue and print the pass.

Limited Time

Initial Free Ride Facility

During the initial phase of the Campus Bus Service, rides are provided free of charge. Users do not need to make any payment to avail the Daily Pass during this period. To access the free ride:

  1. Open the PUBBS Transit Campus App and log in.
  2. Go to the “My Tickets” section and select the Daily Pass.
  3. Select any available payment method and proceed to the payment page.
  4. The Daily Pass is generated without any payment being deducted during the initial free-ride period.
  5. Use the QR code generated with the pass for validation while boarding the bus — scanned through the in-bus QR scanner or by the conductor.

Please carry a digital pass / QR code or printed ticket while travelling. The QR code must be successfully validated through the in-bus QR scanner or by the conductor before travel.
