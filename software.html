Pubbs Transit

PUBBS Transit is an integrated transit planning, operations, and management platform designed to help public transport agencies and bus operators plan services, manage resources, monitor operations, and improve passenger experiences through a unified digital ecosystem.

The platform brings together route planning, scheduling, fleet management, workforce coordination, operational monitoring, forecasting, and passenger services into a single environment, enabling transport organizations to manage the complete service lifecycle more efficiently.

Why It Exists

Purpose

Public transportation operations often rely on disconnected systems, spreadsheets, manual processes, and fragmented data. PUBBS Transit was created to address these challenges by providing a centralized platform that supports planning, execution, monitoring, and continuous optimization of transit services.

The goal is to help operators make better decisions, improve operational efficiency, enhance service reliability, and create a more connected experience for both staff and passengers.

Core Value Proposition

A Single Source of Truth for Transit Operations

PUBBS Transit provides a single source of truth for transit operations.

By connecting planning, scheduling, dispatch, workforce management, operational monitoring, forecasting, and passenger-facing services, the platform enables organizations to reduce operational complexity, improve visibility, and respond faster to changing conditions.

The result is a more reliable, efficient, and data-driven transit operation.

PUBBS Transit is designed as a modern, scalable platform that supports collaboration across multiple operational teams.
